Meet Recorder is ready

Record Google Meet calls and get a transcript with speaker names. Everything stays on this computer.

1Allow your microphone

Chrome lets the extension hear the other participants on its own. To include your voice in recordings, allow microphone access once.

2Record a meeting

  1. Join a call on meet.google.com.
  2. Click the Meet Recorder icon in the toolbar. Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu to keep it handy.
  3. Press Start recording. Meet's captions switch on and become the transcript.
  4. Press Stop & save, or just leave the call. The recording opens with its transcript.

3Optional: AI transcription

Add an OpenAI API key, or the address of a self-hosted Whisper server, in Settings to transcribe recordings from their audio.

Recording laws differ from place to place. Always tell participants before you record.
